# Reverie desktop artwork

The built-in ImageGen tool created the two original landscape and lens sources. The desktop wordmark uses the paths in Reverie's original lettering with a thicker pearl-and-glass finish. Run `uv run --script mac-clap/tools/bake_surface_art.py` to rebuild the embedded artwork.

## Desktop horizon

Use case: stylized-concept. Edit the reference panorama for a desktop synthesizer masthead. Full bleed original early-2000s Frutiger Aero CGI wallpaper, ultrawide horizontal composition. Keep its rich cobalt sky, sunlit green islands, luminous turquoise seawater, polished chromatic glass, tiny futuristic silver spires and water ribbon. Recompose with the main round glass globe (enclosing a miniature sunlit island and city) centered at 48 percent of the width, its diameter just 17 percent of image width; place the horizon and globe center on the vertical middle. Add a small curving chain of verdant islets, wispy cumulus and bead-like bubbles receding across the bay. Keep the left quarter dark cobalt and calm for the white wordmark, the right quarter darker blue and uncluttered for controls. Frame the action in a shallow center strip usable at an 8:1 crop. Please make the widest possible panoramic artwork with careful optical reflections and luminous atmospheric perspective. No typography, no frame, no UI or logos.

## Motion glass

Original square artwork for the interactive X/Y modulation display of the Reverie synthesizer, matching a bright Frutiger Aero glass music player. A single spherical, transparent deep aquamarine optical trackball in a shallow, midnight teal glass recess, viewed exactly straight on. Center the globe horizontally at 50% and vertically at 46%; its diameter is 75% of the image width. The orb has an unmistakable rounded contour, a broad milky sky reflection arcing across its upper left cap, blue-green refraction, subtle reflections of a green island and distant soft cloud along its lower edge. Very fine translucent longitude and latitude lines lightly etched on the orb; the center and area around its silhouette should stay dark enough for a live lime modulation path. Refined original mid-2000s photoreal 3D icon craftsmanship. Clean simple optical shapes readable at 120x118. No text, letters, labels, crosshair, paths, arrows, icons or UI decorations.

## Panel glass

An original Frutiger Aero abstract glass texture for the continuous underside of an aqua desktop music synthesizer control panel, horizontal 16:9. Close-up glossy molded transparent acrylic viewed straight on, gently bending a few translucent ribbons of glacial turquoise water from bottom left to top right, with broad swaths of extremely light celadon green, limpid sky blue, pearlescent white and very subtle cerulean caustic reflections. A sparse group of small realistic clear water beads and elongated specular streaks near the outer edges. Fine details of polished water and glass that would still show when faintly visible through a frosted overlay. Early-2000s optimistic premium audio-player skin. Calm luminous surface, no text, letters, frames, buttons, screens, grid or interface. All of the image is the material texture filling the frame.

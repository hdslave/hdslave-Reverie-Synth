# Reverie screen artwork

[horizon.jpg](horizon.jpg) and [aquarium.jpg](aquarium.jpg) are project-sized sources from the built-in ImageGen tool. [wordmark.svg](wordmark.svg) contains the lettering. The on-screen labels use Barlow SemiBold under the [SIL Open Font License](../licenses/OFL-barlow.txt).

The CLAP embeds the artwork and glyph masks. After changing a source, run `uv run --script tools/bake_screen_art.py` from the project root to rebuild `src/ui/screen_art.h`.

## Panorama prompt

Original Frutiger Aero panoramic landscape artwork for the tiny display of the Reverie music synthesizer. Ultra-wide 3:1 horizontal panorama, no text, typography, UI, borders or logos. Authentic 2005 glossy photorealistic 3D advertising art. Compose all essential motifs inside a shallow horizontal strip around the midline. At left keep the first third simple rich cobalt blue sky fading to teal water, low detail for a white wordmark. At center, sunlit, unbelievably green rounded grassy peninsula and soft white puffy cloud. At center-right one HUGE crystal-clear iridescent glass bubble containing a refracted green hill, a tiny cluster of slender silver-blue futuristic city towers and sky, a few small floating bubbles of different sizes; a lively translucent turquoise water ribbon sweeps along the lower edge and curls up on far right. Vivid cerulean sky, aquamarine shallows, chartreuse turf, pearl-white glints. Bright optimistic daylight, lush landscape textures, clean silhouette, slightly surreal early CGI, luxurious glass caustics. Think nostalgic music-player wallpaper. Composition must read legibly downscaled to a 320-pixel-wide, very shallow banner. No mockup or lettering.

## Aquarium prompt

Original Frutiger Aero aquarium artwork used as a small portrait window on the effects page of a hardware synthesizer. A single elegant orange-and-pearl goldfish with flowing translucent tail, seen SIDE ON clearly swimming to the right at mid-height, body and fins a strong generous silhouette across the middle of the frame. Glassy ascending air bubbles, turquoise to deep sapphire blue water, a few green aquatic leaves in the bottom corners, luminous upward caustics, retro early-2000s photoreal CGI desktop aquarium screensaver, optimistic clean lush glossy materials. Sunbeams from near the surface. Keep the bottom quarter darker and calm for a small live level meter, upper left calm for a label. Full bleed image, approximately square. Only the underwater scene, no picture frame, no text, no interface, no logos.
